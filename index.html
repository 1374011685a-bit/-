04 / CONTROLS

How to play

怎么玩？

PC · 电脑端

SpaceMouse Click

Press Space or click anywhere to charge, release to jump.
按空格或鼠标点击，按住蓄力、松开出跳。

Mobile · 手机端

Touch Tap

Tap anywhere on screen; long-press to charge, release to jump. One-hand friendly.
手指点屏幕，长按蓄力、松开出跳，单手就能玩。

Three steps to get hooked

  2. 2 Dodge躲开缺口与陷阱 · Time it — don't fall, don't hit traps
