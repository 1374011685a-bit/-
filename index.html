04 / CONTROLS

How to play

PC

SpaceMouse Click

Press Space or click anywhere to charge, release to jump.

Mobile

Touch Tap

Tap anywhere on screen; long-press to charge, release to jump. One-hand friendly.

02 / CORE GAMEPLAY

Three steps to get hooked

  1. 1 JumpHold to charge, release to leap to the next platform
  2. 2 DodgeTime it — don't fall, don't hit traps
  3. 3 CollectGrab every star on the way

You jump from exactly where you land, so coming down further right makes the next jump shorter. Land on the far edge and it counts as a PERFECT landing, worth +50.

06 / COLLECTIBLES

Power-ups

ShieldBlocks one fall or crash
BoostJump farther and faster for a while
MagnetPulls in nearby stars

Stars are both your score and your currency — spend them on new skins in the shop. Holding a charge never wastes it: the power meter swings back down, so you can wait for a moving platform to come close.
